Keyboard

Your hands never leave the home row.

Every action in the inbox is one key. Move, star, archive, reply, undo — a week of messages goes by without reaching for the trackpad.

inƒlow Focused 6 conversations
    J K move · S star · U unread · E archive · Z undo
    J

    Muscle memory, borrowed.

    The bindings come from the mail clients you already know — J and K to move, E to archive, Z to undo. Destructive actions are always undoable, so you can triage fast without being careful.

    Local-first

    There is no inƒlow server.

    No account to create, no backend to trust, no analytics. inƒlow reads your messages with the LinkedIn session already in your browser and stores them in IndexedDB on your own machine, partitioned per account. The developer cannot see your data, because there is nowhere for it to go.

    HostWhyWhat is sent
    www.linkedin.com Read and send your messages The LinkedIn session cookies already in your browser
    media.licdn.com Profile photos for desktop notifications Nothing but the image URL
    generativelanguage.googleapis.com Optional AI replies — off until you add a key The open conversation, under your own Gemini key
    api.github.com Check whether a newer version shipped Nothing about you — a public release lookup
    • No selling or sharing of data
    • No advertising or profiling
    • No telemetry or crash reporting
    • Uninstalling deletes everything

    Search

    Find it the way you remember it.

    Local search filters as you type, across every message already on your machine — then hands off to LinkedIn's own search when you need to go further back.

    is:unreadis:readis:starredis:group has:drafthas:attachmentfrom:name after:2026-01-01before:2026-01-01 newer:7dolder:30d

    Autocomplete built in

    Start typing a filter and finish it with Tab or Enter. The rest of the query stays free text.

    Matches, highlighted

    Results show the matched words in context inside the preview line, not just the sender's name.

    Deeper on demand

    Paginated server-side search reaches conversations that haven't been backfilled yet.

    Sync

    Already there when you open it.

    A background service worker keeps a live stream open and backfills history in priority order, so the inbox is warm before you arrive.

    Realtime, then polling

    Server-sent events deliver new messages the moment they land, with a 30-second poll underneath as a safety net.

    Send before the network

    Messages appear instantly and reconcile on confirmation. Actions taken offline queue up and replay when you're back.

    Notifications that land right

    Native Chrome notifications carry the sender's photo, and clicking one opens that exact thread.

    Install

    One click from the Chrome Web Store.

    Add to Chrome Installed View in Chrome Store
    01

    Add it

    Open the listing and hit Add to Chrome. Pin the icon while you're there — inƒlow opens from the toolbar.

    02

    Be signed into LinkedIn

    In any tab. inƒlow uses the session you already have; there is no inƒlow account to create.

    03

    Open it

    Click the icon and the inbox fills itself, newest conversations first, while history backfills behind you.
